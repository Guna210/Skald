# Public-Domain EPUB Text Extraction Review

Generated by `core/tests/golden/make_golden_text.py` from the two public-domain EPUB fixtures so you can check extraction quality in about 10 minutes.

## What to look for

- **Wrong chapter breaks:** chapters split in the middle of a story, merged together, or titled with body text instead of a chapter heading.
- **Missing or duplicated text:** missing opening or closing lines, cut-off sentences, or text repeated across chapter boundaries.
- **Page numbers or notes read aloud:** stray page numbers, running headers/footers, footnote markers, or footnote/endnote bodies appearing in the story.
- **Odd characters:** garbled symbols, broken quotes or apostrophes, leftover HTML markup, or unusual spacing and line breaks.

## Known issues (already noted — no need to report)

- **Gutenberg header, credits, and licence chapters:** Project Gutenberg includes front-matter and licence pages in the spine, so they appear as chapters (users can untick them in the Book screen).
- **Untagged `Contents` and `ILLUSTRATIONS` tables:** These pages in the source EPUBs have no `toc` or `landmarks` tags, so Skald does not auto-skip them unless they fall under the 200-character `short` rule.
- ***A Christmas Carol*'s `div.fig` captions mid-stave:** Illustration captions in this EPUB use non-standard `<div class="fig">` elements rather than `<figcaption>`, so they stay in the extracted text; some are also TOC anchor targets, which splits a stave at the illustration and places the caption at the boundary.
- **Empty cover wrapper pages:** Cover-only XHTML pages extract 0 paragraphs and are marked `suggested-skip (short)`.
